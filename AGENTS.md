## Learned User Preferences

- The website is the local `redesign/` pages. An Unicorn Studio scene already open in the browser, including a portrait or image treatment, is not the site reference.
- Motion on the site should be a lavender silk animation in the brand colors, especially on very white pages, so sections have contrast and information stays distinct.

## Learned Workspace Facts

- The redesign is static HTML in `redesign/`: `index.html`, `services.html`, `about.html`, `contact.html`, `obituaries.html`, per-obituary pages under `redesign/obituary/`, and styles in `redesign/assets/`.
- Brand colors live in `redesign/assets/tokens.css`: plum (`--c-plum`), lavender (`--c-lavender`), lilac (`--c-lilac`), and a near-white page background (`--c-bg`).
- `redesign/services.html` is the long white Services page; service groups stack on one white field and need visual separation.
- Impeccable Live’s `localhost:8400/live.js` is the helper script, not a page. Open the redesign HTML pages to see the site.
